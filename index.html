About the Cabinet

Paddy's Whiskey Cabinet is a monthly whiskey tasting group hosted at Paddy's Pub where friends gather to explore a new theme each month.

Some nights feature smoky Scotch. Others focus on Irish whiskey, bourbon, or whatever sounds interesting.

The theme is announced ahead of time. The bottles stay a surprise until tasting night.
